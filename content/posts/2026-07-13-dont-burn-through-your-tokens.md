I originally put this together as a one-pager for the AI Guild at work, to help people pick the right Claude model without overthinking it. The arcade analogy stuck, so I'm reposting it here. Also this really applies to all AIs, I just wrote it "for Claude" since that's what my audience uses.

Think of AI usage like an arcade. Every session, you're inserting tokens. The claw machine costs **1 token per play**, but the full-on VR racing simulator costs **25**. The VR rig is more impressive, sure, but if you just want to grab some candy, it's complete overkill. Claude works the same way: four model tiers, each one significantly pricier than the last. Matching the right model to your task keeps everyone's budget healthy.

## Quick reference — pick your machine

| Tier & Model | Relative&nbsp;Cost | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Basic**<br>_Haiku 4.5_ | ●○○○○<br>1×&nbsp;baseline | Simple Q&A, quick lookups, translations, short summaries, reformatting text or data |
| **Standard**<br>_Sonnet 5_ | ●●○○○<br>~4×&nbsp;baseline | Writing & editing, analysis, coding help, brainstorming, research — most everyday work |
| **Advanced**<br>_Opus 4.8_ | ●●●●○<br>~15×&nbsp;baseline | Complex multi-step reasoning, deep research, nuanced long-form writing, hard debugging |
| **Premium**<br>_Fable 5_ | ●●●●●<br>~25×&nbsp;baseline | Cutting-edge problems, highest-stakes creative or strategic work, when Advanced still falls short |

## When in doubt, start with Standard (Sonnet 5)

It handles the vast majority of work (writing, analysis, coding, brainstorming) without burning through your budget. Step up to Advanced or Premium only when you've hit a wall.

**Hot tip:** you can always upgrade mid-conversation without starting over.

*Model names and relative costs current as of mid-2025.*
